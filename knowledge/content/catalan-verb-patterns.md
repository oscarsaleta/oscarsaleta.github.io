# Catalan Verb Patterns & Mnemonic Anchors

High-frequency irregular patterns, subjunctive triggers, and structural anchors for learning Catalan.

---

## The Three Regular Conjugations (Present Tense)

| Pronoun | 1st (-ar: *parlar*) | 2nd (-re/-er: *perdre*) | 3rd (-ir: *dormir*) | 3rd Inchoative (-ir: *servir*) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Jo** | parl**o** | perd**o** | dorm**o** | serv**eixo** |
| **Tu** | parl**es** | perd**s** | dorm**s** | serv**eixes** |
| **Ell / Ella** | parl**a** | perd | dorm | serv**eix** |
| **Nosaltres**| parl**em** | perd**em** | dorm**im** | serv**im** |
| **Vosaltres**| parl**eu** | perd**eu** | dorm**iu** | serv**iu** |
| **Ells / Elles**| parl**en** | perd**en** | dorm**en** | serv**eixen** |

> **Inchoative Note**: Most modern `-ir` verbs in Catalan (like *preferir, servir, construir, conduir*) follow the `-eix-` inchoative paradigm for singular persons and 3rd person plural.

## High-Frequency Irregulars: *Anar, Ser, Estar, Tenir, Fer*

### Present Indicative Matrix

- **Ser** (to be - identity/inherent): *sóc, ets, és, som, sou, són*
- **Estar** (to be - state/location): *estic, estàs, està, estem, esteu, estan*
- **Anar** (to go): *vaig, vas, va, anem, aneu, van*
- **Tenir** (to have): *tinc, tens, té, tenim, teniu, tenen*
- **Fer** (to do/make): *faig, fas, fa, fem, feu, fan*

## Past Tense Mastery: The Periphrastic Preterite (*El Pretèrit Perfet Perifràstic*)

In Catalan, spoken past actions almost universally use the auxiliary verb **anar** combined with the **infinitive**, NOT the compound "have done" auxiliary!

$$\text{Pretèrit Perifràstic} = \text{Auxiliary Form} + \text{Infinitive}$$

| Person | Auxiliary Form | Example (*menjar* = to eat) | English Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jo** | **vaig** | vaig menjar | I ate |
| **Tu** | **vas** | vas menjar | you ate |
| **Ell/Ella**| **va** | va menjar | he/she ate |
| **Nosaltres**| **vam** *(or vàrem)* | vam menjar | we ate |
| **Vosaltres**| **vau** *(or vàreu)* | vau menjar | you all ate |
| **Ells/Elles**| **van** | van menjar | they ate |

> **Pro Tip**: Notice the distinct forms *vam* and *vau* for the periphrastic past (versus *anem* and *aneu* in the present tense).
