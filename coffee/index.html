Percolation vs. Pressurized Extraction

Extraction Parameter Pour-Over (Filter) Espresso
Driving Force Atmospheric gravity + hydrostatic head 6 to 9 bars pump pressure
Typical Grind Particle Size Medium-fine to medium (500–800 μm) Fine to extra-fine (150–350 μm)
Standard Brew Ratio 1:15 to 1:17 (e.g. 15g → 240g) 1:1.5 to 1:3 (e.g. 18g → 36g)
Extraction Contact Time 2:00 to 4:00 minutes 12 to 35 seconds
TDS (Total Dissolved Solids) 1.2% – 1.5% (Clean, delicate clarity) 8% – 12% (Viscous, emulsified crema)
Primary Channeling Cause Over-fining and excessive drawdown swirling Puck clumps, uneven tamping, high resistance
