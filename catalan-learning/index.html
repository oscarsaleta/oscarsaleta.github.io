Essential Grammar Rules

🏷️ Definite Articles (Els articles definits)

the
Gender Singular Plural Before Vowel / H
Masculine el els l'
Feminine la les l'
📌 Apostrophization: Use l' before masculine words starting with a vowel or 'h' (e.g., l'home, l'amic) and feminine words starting with a vowel/h (e.g., l'aigua, l'hora), except unstressed i/u (e.g., la universitat).

🤝 Noun & Adjective Agreement

Adjectives in Catalan agree in both gender (masculine / feminine) and number (singular / plural) with the noun they describe.

Form Masculine Feminine English Translation
Singular el cotxe blanc la casa blanca the white car / the white house
Plural els cotxes blancs les cases blanques the white cars / the white houses
