Essential Grammar Rules

Definite Articles (Els articles definits)

Singular Plural
Masculine el / l' els
Feminine la / l' les

Use l' before a word starting with a vowel or 'h'.

Noun & Adjective Agreement

Adjectives must agree in gender and number with the noun they describe.

Masculine Feminine
Singular el cotxe blanc (the white car) la casa blanca (the white house)
Plural els cotxes blancs (the white cars) les cases blanques (the white houses)
