Core Nutrition Principles

Energy Balance & Availability

Consume enough calories to match your high expenditure from endurance running and cross-training. Chronic low energy availability (LEA) suppresses endocrine recovery, compromises bone density, and spikes injury risk.

Nutrient Timing

Prioritize easily digestible carbohydrates before and during workouts for immediate glycogen replenishment, followed by a 3:1 to 4:1 carb-to-protein recovery meal within 45 minutes of finishing.

Plant-Based Nutrient Density

Focus on unprocessed, micronutrient-dense plant sources: legumes, whole grains, seeds, and vibrant dark leafy vegetables to supply high polyphenols and anti-inflammatory compounds.

Gut Adaptation Consistency

Intestinal transport enzymes (SGLT1 and GLUT5) upregulate with habitual exposure. Train your stomach to tolerate high carb concentrations on all weekly long runs.

Vegetarian Macronutrients

🧱

Protein: The Builder

Target 1.4 – 1.8g per kg body weight daily for muscle tissue repair and immune resilience.

Key Sources:
  • • Lentils, Tempeh & Firm Tofu
  • • Edamame & Seitan (High Leucine)
  • • Quinoa & Hemp / Chia Seeds
  • • Pea / Rice Protein Isolates
⚡

Carbohydrates: The Fuel

Primary engine for high-output aerobic efforts. Emphasize complex carbs in daily diet, simple sugars during runs.

Key Sources:
  • • Rolled Oats & Sourdough Bread
  • • Sweet Potatoes, Squash & Beets
  • • Brown Rice, Polenta & Buckwheat
  • • Bananas, Medjool Dates & Berries
🥑

Fats: The Sustainer

Crucial for steroid hormone synthesis, fat-soluble vitamin uptake (A, D, E, K), and base metabolic oxidation.

Key Sources:
  • • Extra Virgin Olive Oil & Avocados
  • • Walnuts & Raw Almonds
  • • Ground Flaxseed & Pumpkin Seeds
  • • Tahini & Natural Peanut Butter

Micronutrients & Electrolytes

Iron & Ferritin

Essential for hemoglobin and cellular oxygen transport. Pair non-heme plant iron (lentils, spinach) with Vitamin C (citrus, peppers) and avoid tannins/coffee around iron-rich meals.

Vitamin B12

Vital for nerve transmission and erythropoiesis (red blood cell production). Non-negotiable daily supplementation (cyanocobalamin or methylcobalamin) for vegetarian athletes.

Calcium & Vitamin D3

Structural foundation for bone remodeling under repetitive ultra running ground reaction forces. Fortified plant milks and sensible sun exposure or D3 drops.

Sodium & Potassium

Maintains extracellular fluid balance and action potential in skeletal muscle. Sweat sodium loss ranges from 800–1200mg/L; replenish with targeted electrolyte caps.

Magnesium

Cofactor in ATP synthesis and neuromuscular relaxation. Rich in pumpkin seeds, leafy greens, dark chocolate, and whole grains.

Zinc

Supports immune barrier integrity and cellular repair following systemic muscular breakdown during multi-hour efforts.

💧 Hydration Strategy

  • • Baseline: Drink 2.5–3.5 liters of fluid daily, monitoring first-morning urine color (pale straw target).
  • • Pre-Race: 500ml water with 500mg sodium 90 minutes before race start; stop drinking 30 min before to empty bladder.
  • • During Effort: Small frequent sips (150–200ml every 15–20 minutes) rather than large volumes that slosh in the stomach.
  • • Post-Run: Replace 125–150% of body mass lost in sweat over 2–4 hours with sodium-rich broth or electrolyte fluids.

🏔️ 100km Ultra Protocols

  • • Carb Loading: Increase carbohydrate intake to 8–10g/kg body weight for 36–48h pre-race without excess fiber.
  • • Start Fueling Early: Take your first gel or fuel at minute 30–45. Once you fall behind on glycogen, catch-up is physiologically impossible.
  • • Palate Fatigue Plan: After 6–8 hours, sweet flavors nauseate the palate. Pack salted boiled potatoes, pretzels, or pickled ginger.
  • • Emergency Aid: Keep 100mg caffeine gel in reserve for severe mental dips between kilometer 70 and 85.
